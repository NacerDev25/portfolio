Frequently asked questions

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. A single page usually takes a few days, while a full site takes one to three weeks.

Do you work with existing designs?

Yes. I can build from a design you already have, or help shape the structure before development starts.

Will the site work on mobile?

Yes. Every page is built to reflow and stay usable at small screen sizes.
